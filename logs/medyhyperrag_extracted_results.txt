In the medical domain, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) has been applied to mitigate the limitations of large

RAG(KG-RAG)addressesthesechallengesbyintegratingstructuredentity–relationknowledge,enablingmorespecificand

Experiments on the RJUA-QA dataset show that Medical HyperRAG outperforms existing RAG paradigms—such as
