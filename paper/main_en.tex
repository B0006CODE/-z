\begin{abstract}
Medical retrieval-augmented generation systems require evidence retrieval that is both accurate and medically grounded. This paper presents KCH-MedRank, a lightweight and reproducible knowledge-constrained local hypergraph learning-to-rank framework for biomedical evidence retrieval and reranking. Enhanced candidate generation improves the top-100 evidence ceiling, and KCH-MedRank improves top-10 evidence retrieval over strong Hybrid RRF and MedCPT cross-encoder baselines on the held-out BioASQ test split. PubMedQA answer-support analysis shows high citation support for retrieved evidence but limited answer-label support for lightweight answer selection, so answer generation is treated as a secondary component rather than the main contribution.
\end{abstract}
