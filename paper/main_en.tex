\begin{abstract}
Medical retrieval-augmented generation systems require evidence retrieval that is both accurate and medically grounded. This manuscript studies KCH-MedRank, a lightweight and reproducible knowledge-constrained local hypergraph learning-to-rank framework for biomedical evidence retrieval and reranking. The current draft is a bilingual LaTeX skeleton. It records the verified experimental main line: enhanced candidate generation improves the top-100 evidence ceiling, and KCH-MedRank improves top-10 evidence retrieval over strong Hybrid RRF and MedCPT cross-encoder baselines on the held-out BioASQ test split. Answer generation remains secondary until controlled answer and evidence-support metrics are complete.
\end{abstract}
