\section{Ablation Study}

The ablation study should separate retrieval-strength gains from medical-knowledge and hypergraph contributions. Current result files include removal settings for the biomedical semantic score, MeSH hierarchy features, biomedical entity features, hypergraph diffusion and centrality, and PrimeKG relation features.

Preliminary interpretation is conservative. Biomedical semantic and retrieval features are strong. MeSH hierarchy and shared concept clusters provide medically interpretable features, but some leave-one-out ablations have small or mixed differences. PrimeKG exact-name relation features have low coverage and should remain auxiliary unless future normalization improves their contribution.
