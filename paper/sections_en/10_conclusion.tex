\section{Conclusion}

This manuscript skeleton positions KCH-MedRank as a lightweight, reproducible, knowledge-constrained local hypergraph learning-to-rank framework for biomedical evidence retrieval and reranking. The current BioASQ results show stronger top-10 evidence retrieval than original Hybrid RRF and a true MedCPT Cross-Encoder baseline on the same enhanced candidate pool, with statistically significant Recall@10 improvement over the semantic reranker. Future work should finalize ablations, method diagrams, efficiency analysis, secondary-dataset robustness, and controlled answer-generation evaluation.
