\subsection{Interpretability and Error Analysis}
\label{sec:interpretability}

A reranker that improves numbers is useful. One that can explain its decisions is necessary for a medical setting where a clinician may need to verify why a particular passage was promoted. KCH-MedRank is built so that LambdaMART feature importance scores and hypergraph pathways make each ranking decision traceable. This section gives the quantitative evidence, then looks at failure cases with equal care.

\subsubsection{Three Mechanisms Behind Evidence Rescue}

We classified all 648 gold passages that KCH-MedRank rescues from outside the Hybrid RRF top-10 into the top-10. Each rescue was assigned to the dominant hypergraph mechanism that drove it.

The MeSH hierarchy path accounts for 75.9\% of rescues with an average rank gain of +22.1 positions. Here the question and the gold passage share MeSH concepts through hierarchical tree ancestor paths. A question about DITPA and a passage about thyroid hormones connect through the shared MeSH term ``thyroid hormones'' and its tree ancestors.

Shared entity cluster paths account for 23.9\% of rescues with an average gain of +14.0 positions. The gold passage shares biomedical entities with other highly-ranked candidate passages. Shared-entity hyperedge clusters propagate ranking signal through diffusion.

PrimeKG relation paths account for 0.2\% of rescues. Question entities connect to passage entities through PrimeKG biomedical relations (e.g., ``Dasatinib --treats-- Leukemia''). The low share reflects sparse exact-name mapping in PrimeKG.

The strongest rescue involved p-crAssphage with a rank gain of +56. A gold passage about bacteriophage biology moved from rank 54 to rank 1. The main alignment signal was the shared bacteriophage concept supported by a local cluster of phage-related candidates. MeSH and hypergraph features promoted evidence that was medically aligned even though the enhanced hybrid ordering had buried it outside the top 10.

The strongest MeSH-hierarchy rescue moved a passage about the cGAS pathway by +83 ranks. The question ``What is the function of the cGAS pathway?'' shares multiple immunological MeSH terms with competing candidates. A dense hierarchy-based cluster formed and hypergraph diffusion propagated it effectively.

Failures cluster around a common pattern. In the lost ubiquitination case, a gold passage about mono-ubiquitinated and poly-ubiquitinated LDH dropped from rank 2 to rank 18. The question and passage use related biochemical terminology, but synonym and hierarchy features cannot bridge the surface forms to broader post-translational-processing concepts. Similar losses occur for thyroid hormone analogs, where DITPA variants are not captured by MeSH synonymy; cilengitide receptors, where integrin terminology is inconsistent; dasatinib treatment, where drug-indication mapping has gaps; GIST treatment, where rare disease concept coverage is thin; and Fanconi anemia, where DNA repair pathway synonymy fails. Every one of these lost cases points toward the same future direction: biomedical concept normalization that handles synonyms and abbreviations would close the largest remaining gap.

\subsubsection{What the Interpretability Evidence Shows}

The three-mechanism analysis gives quantitative backing for the claim that KCH-MedRank's decisions follow medically grounded pathways.

Every rescued passage activates at least one knowledge feature group. There are zero pure diffusion-only rescues. The MeSH hierarchy path is the most frequently dominant pathway at 75.9\%. Multiple concurrent knowledge pathways are active in 89.2\% of rescues. MeSH-driven rescues produce substantially larger average rank gains than entity-driven rescues (+22.1 vs.\ +14.0), which suggests hierarchical concept alignment carries more ranking signal than flat entity co-occurrence.

A methodological caveat: the mechanism classification uses the same feature scores that LambdaMART consumes. It identifies which features are most strongly activated, not which causally drive the ranking. Causal attribution with methods like SHAP would strengthen this claim in future work.
